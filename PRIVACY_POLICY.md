## 2. Information We Collect

Saanvi collects only the information needed to provide and protect the bot's features.

### Discord Information

When you interact with Saanvi, we may process:

- Discord user IDs.
- Discord server IDs.
- Discord channel IDs.
- Discord usernames, tags, display names, and avatar information when needed to show responses or support tickets.
- Discord command interactions, button clicks, select menu choices, modal submissions, and message content used to run prefix commands.
- Discord permission and server context needed to decide whether commands can run.

### User-Saved Profile Information

If you choose to save payment profile information, Saanvi stores it under your Discord user ID. This may include:

- Cryptocurrency wallet addresses.
- UPI IDs.
- PayPal usernames.

You can remove saved profile information using the relevant remove commands, such as `removeaddy`, `removeupi`, and `removepaypal`.

### Server Configuration

For Discord servers using Saanvi, we may store:

- Server ID.
- Command prefixes.
- Ignored channel IDs.
- Custom profile status.
- Timestamps for custom profile updates, such as avatar, banner, or bio update times.

### Premium and Access Records

Saanvi may store:

- Premium expiration timestamps linked to Discord user IDs.
- Premium code IDs, duration, expiration time, creator Discord user ID, redeemer Discord user ID, and redemption timestamp.
- Blacklist records for users or servers, including Discord IDs, reason, type, creator ID, and timestamp.

### Support Tickets

If you create a support ticket, Saanvi may process:

- Your Discord user ID and tag.
- Ticket category.
- Ticket message and replies.
- Ticket ID and ticket state.

Support ticket data may be cached temporarily so the owner can respond.

### AI Questions

If you use the `ask` command, Saanvi processes:

- The question you submit.
- Whether you enabled web-assisted answering.
- The generated answer and basic timing/status information needed to respond.

Questions may be sent to a third-party AI provider to generate an answer. If web-assisted answering is enabled, the provider may also use search or web retrieval features according to the provider configuration.

### Crypto and Blockchain Lookups

When you use price, balance, or transaction commands, Saanvi may process:

- Cryptocurrency symbols or network choices.
- Wallet addresses.
- Transaction hashes.
- Public blockchain data returned by third-party providers.

Public blockchain data is generally public by design. Avoid submitting wallet addresses that you do not want associated with your Discord use.

### Technical Logs and Cache

Saanvi may create logs and temporary cache entries for:

- Error diagnosis.
- Rate limits and cooldowns.
- Performance, latency, and uptime checks.
- Abuse prevention and security.
- Temporary feature state, such as support ticket state.

Logs may include command names, Discord IDs, errors, timestamps, and limited command context.

## 3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Saanvi does not intentionally ask for or require:

- Passwords.
- Discord tokens.
- Private keys, seed phrases, or recovery phrases.
- Full payment card numbers.
- Government identification numbers.
- Sensitive personal information unrelated to the bot's features.

Do not send this information to Saanvi. If you accidentally submit highly sensitive information, contact us and delete the message from Discord where possible.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Run Saanvi commands and interactions.
- Save and display user payment profiles when requested.
- Look up cryptocurrency prices, balances, and transactions.
- Provide AI-generated answers.
- Manage premium access and premium codes.
- Provide support tickets and owner replies.
- Store server configuration.
- Enforce cooldowns, prevent abuse, investigate errors, and protect Saanvi.
- Comply with Discord rules, applicable laws, and valid legal requests.

## 5. Legal Bases Where Required

Where a legal basis is required, we process information based on one or more of the following:

- Performance of a service you requested, such as running a command or saving a profile value.
- Legitimate interests, such as security, abuse prevention, debugging, and maintaining bot functionality.
- Consent, where you choose to submit optional information such as saved payment handles or AI questions.
- Legal obligations, where we must comply with applicable law or valid legal process.

## 6. How We Share Information

We may share or transmit information with:

- Discord, because Saanvi operates through Discord and uses Discord APIs.
- Hosting, database, logging, and infrastructure providers used to run Saanvi.
- Tatum or other blockchain data providers used for balance and transaction lookups.
- Market data or chart providers used for price commands.
- OpenRouter or other AI providers used for the `ask` command.
- Public blockchain explorers or APIs when generating links or lookups.
- The bot owner or support staff when handling support tickets, abuse reports, premium requests, or operational issues.
- Legal, safety, or platform authorities when required by law, Discord rules, or to protect users and the Service.

We do not sell Discord user data. We do not share Discord user data with advertising networks or data brokers.

## 7. Third-Party Services

Third-party services process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. Saanvi is not responsible for the privacy practices of Discord, AI providers, blockchain providers, hosting providers, payment providers, or other third-party services.

## 8. Data Retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this Policy, unless a longer period is required for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, backups, or dispute handling.

Current expected retention:

- Saved payment profile data: kept until you remove it or request deletion.
- Server configuration: kept while Saanvi is used in the server or until deletion is requested.
- Premium status: kept while needed to provide premium access.
- Premium codes: code records may expire automatically after their expiration time.
- Support ticket cache: intended to expire after approximately 30 days.
- Blacklist records: kept while needed to protect Saanvi and enforce access restrictions.
- Logs and temporary cache: kept for a limited period based on operational needs.

Backup copies may persist for a limited time after deletion before they are overwritten.

## 9. Your Choices and Controls

- Stop using Saanvi at any time.
- Remove Saanvi from a Discord server if you have the required server permissions.

- Ask us to access, correct, or delete information linked to your Discord user ID.
- Ask us to delete server configuration if you are authorized to manage the relevant Discord server.

Deletion requests should include your Discord user ID and enough detail for us to find the relevant data.
